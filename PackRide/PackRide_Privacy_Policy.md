# PackRide Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 17, 2026**

This policy describes what information PackRide ("the App") collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. PackRide is a motorcycle-riding social app that helps riders track rides, ride together in groups, join communities, and share rides with people who follow them.

## Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password (the password itself is never stored by us — it's handled directly by our authentication provider, Firebase Authentication, in encrypted form).

**Profile information.** Your display name, motorcycle, city, and riding experience level, which you enter yourself and which other riders can see.

**Precise location.** PackRide uses your device's GPS location to record your rides, show your live position to other riders during group rides and while riding in a community you've joined, calculate speed and distance, and look up the current speed limit and road name near you. Location is also used, with your permission, to detect nearby riders and alert you to them.

**Motion and fitness data.** PackRide uses your device's motion sensors (accelerometer) during a ride to detect sudden impacts consistent with a crash, so it can alert your group or community. Raw motion data stays on your device and is never transmitted or stored anywhere — only a crash *alert* (not the underlying sensor data) is sent if a crash is detected.

**Ride and route data.** Distance, duration, maximum speed, and GPS route (GPX) for each ride you record. This is stored on your device. If you choose to post a ride to the Ride Feed, a simplified version of the route, along with the ride's title, distance, and duration, is stored on our servers and shown to people who follow you.

**Content you create.** Ride Feed posts, comments, and likes are stored on our servers and visible to other users according to the app's follow and community rules.

**Contacts (optional, on-device only).** If you choose to add an Emergency Contact, PackRide can open your device's built-in Contacts picker so you can select a name and phone number. This information is stored only on your device (not uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party) and is used solely to display your emergency contact within the app.

**Device identifiers and push notification tokens.** PackRide uses a device identifier to manage community membership and a push notification token (issued by Apple/Firebase) so we can notify you when a friend or community member starts a ride, if you've enabled notifications.

**Weather data.** To show weather along your route, PackRide sends route coordinates to Apple's WeatherKit service. This is governed by Apple's own privacy practices for WeatherKit.

**Speed limit data.** PackRide looks up posted speed limits from OpenStreetMap's public Overpass API using anonymous location coordinates — no personal information is sent with these requests.

## How We Use Information

We use the information above to: operate core app features (ride tracking, group rides, communities, the Ride Feed); show your live location to people you're riding with; send notifications about friends' and community members' rides; and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for advertising.

## Who We Share Information With

- **Firebase (Google)** — our backend provider, used for account authentication, data storage (Realtime Database), and push notification delivery.
- **Apple (WeatherKit)** — for weather data along your route.
- **OpenStreetMap Overpass API** — for anonymous, non-personal speed limit lookups.
- **Other PackRide users** — your profile info, live location during shared rides/communities, and any Ride Feed posts you choose to share are visible to riders you're connected with, per the app's follow and community features.

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

## Your Choices

- **Location and motion permissions** can be turned off at any time in your device's Settings, though core ride-tracking and crash-detection features won't work without them.
- **Push notifications** can be turned off in your device's Settings.
- **Account deletion:** you can permanently delete your PackRide account and profile at any time from Profile → Delete Account within the app. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Children's Privacy

PackRide is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
